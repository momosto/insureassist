# InsureHub products

- **Motor insurance:** comprehensive, third party fire and theft, or third party only, for private cars, business vehicles and kombis. Premiums in USD or ZiG (ZWG).
- **Funeral plans:** Standard and Premium plans covering the policyholder, spouse, children and extended family.
- **Home insurance:** buildings and contents, including solar systems and inverters.
- **Credit life:** included with InsureHub Microfinance loans.
- **Microfinance loans:** Salary Advance, Trader and Group (solidarity) loans.

For a quote, use the InsureHub website or ask the assistant to connect you to an agent.
