# Repaying your InsureHub Microfinance loan

You can repay by EcoCash or OneMoney (on WhatsApp, USSD *263# or at a branch), by payroll deduction if you are salaried, or in cash at a branch.

Payments go first to penalties, then fees, credit life, interest and principal, oldest instalment first. If you pay more than is due, the extra is kept as a credit and used for your next instalment.

Early settlement: you can pay off your loan at any time. You pay the outstanding principal, interest up to the day you settle and any settlement fee; future interest is not charged. Ask for a settlement quote first.

If you are struggling to pay, talk to us early — a restructure may be possible.
