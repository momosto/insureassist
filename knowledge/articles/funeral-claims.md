# Funeral claims

We are sorry for your loss. Funeral claims are always handled by a person, not the assistant.

Documents needed: the death certificate or burial order, the policyholder's ID, the deceased's ID or birth certificate, and banking or EcoCash details for payment. Once documents are verified, most funeral claims are paid within 48 hours.

Waiting periods: accidental death is covered from the first day. Natural causes are covered after a 6-month waiting period from the start (or reinstatement) of the plan.
