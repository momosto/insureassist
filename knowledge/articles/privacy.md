# Privacy: how this assistant uses your information

This WhatsApp assistant is an AI service operated by InsureHub Group. It uses an AI model provided by a third party (Anthropic) that processes messages outside Zimbabwe. We only share what is needed to answer you; we do not send your national ID number to the AI model.

Chat transcripts are kept for 12 months and then deleted. You can ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be deleted, by typing "agent". We process your data under the Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07].
