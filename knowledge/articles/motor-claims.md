# Reporting a motor claim

Report an accident as soon as you can, and within 30 days.

You will need: the date and place of the accident, what happened, photos of the damage, the police report reference (required for theft and for any accident involving another vehicle or injury), and the driver's licence. You can start a claim on WhatsApp and send photos; we create a draft and a claims officer contacts you within 2 working days. An assessor inspects the vehicle at an approved panel beater before repairs are authorised. Do not start repairs before the assessor has seen the vehicle. The excess shown on your schedule applies to each claim.
