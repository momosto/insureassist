# Complaints and talking to a person

You can talk to a person at any time: type "agent" (or "munhu" in Shona, "umuntu" in isiNdebele) in the chat, call the contact centre on 0242 700 700 (Mon–Sat, 08:00–18:00), or visit a branch.

Complaints are always handled by a person. We acknowledge complaints within 2 working days and aim to resolve them within 14 days. If you are not satisfied, you may escalate to the Insurance and Pensions Commission (IPEC) for insurance matters or to the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe for microfinance matters.
