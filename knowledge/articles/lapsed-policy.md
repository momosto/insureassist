# My policy has lapsed — how do I get cover back?

A policy lapses when premiums are three months overdue. While it is lapsed there is no cover, and claims for events during the lapse are not paid.

To reinstate it, pay the arrears shown for your policy. Cover restarts the day the payment is received; it is not backdated. For motor policies lapsed for more than 6 months a vehicle inspection may be needed. Funeral plans that are reinstated restart the waiting period for natural causes.
