# How to pay your premium

You can pay InsureHub premiums by EcoCash, OneMoney, bank transfer or at any InsureHub branch.

- **On WhatsApp:** ask the assistant to pay. It shows the amount and policy, then asks you to reply with a 4-digit confirmation code before anything is charged. You then approve the EcoCash prompt on your phone with your EcoCash PIN.
- **On USSD:** dial *263# and choose "Pay premium".
- **Debit order:** ask a branch to set one up.

Premiums are due monthly on the date shown on your policy schedule. We never ask for your EcoCash PIN in a chat or call.
